**Design brief analysis:**
- **Product:** Storefront — a tool for small sellers to create a product listing page, share one link, and receive orders via WhatsApp
- **Audience:** Nigerian/African micro-businesses, market traders going digital, side-hustle sellers, creators
- **Single job of the landing page:** Convince a seller they can go from zero to selling in 2 minutes
- **Tone:** Trustworthy, fast, mobile-native, modern without being corporate

**Design plan:**

*Color palette:* Anchored in a deep forest green (`#1A3C34`) as the primary — evokes commerce, trust, money, and growth without being a typical SaaS blue. A warm off-white (`#F7F4EE`) as the background. WhatsApp's green (`#25D366`) used *only* for WhatsApp-specific CTAs so it carries meaning. A warm amber (`#E8A838`) as the accent — energetic, market-stall warmth. Neutral text in near-black (`#141414`).

*Typography:* `Plus Jakarta Sans` for everything — it's modern, has personality, reads beautifully on mobile, and feels "built for Africa" without being a cliché. Weight contrast does the work instead of a second typeface.

*Signature:* A "one link preview" mockup in the hero — a tiny phone showing a live storefront — making the product tangible before a word is read.

**Color Palette**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Green Deep | `#1A3C34` | Primary — brand, headers, CTAs |
| Green Mid | `#2A5C4E` | Hover states |
| WA Green | `#25D366` | WhatsApp *only* — carries semantic meaning |
| Amber | `#E8A838` | Accent — energy, market warmth, featured actions |
| Background | `#F7F4EE` | Warm off-white, never cold |
| Ink | `#141414` | Body text |

The deep forest green evokes trust, money, and growth without being a generic SaaS blue. The amber adds warmth and feels right for a marketplace product targeting African sellers.

---

**Typography**

Single typeface: **Plus Jakarta Sans** — modern, African-friendly, excellent weight range. Weight contrast (400/600/800) does all the visual hierarchy work instead of mixing families. Clean and fast-loading.
